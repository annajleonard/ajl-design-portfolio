Sip & Stir Cocktail Mixers

Services

Brand Identity, Package Design

Overview

Sip & Stir is a playful mixology brand that began as a mobile cocktail-making app and expanded to include a line of canned cocktail mixers. The packaging extends the brand beyond the screen, connecting digital recipe discovery with a tangible product experience. Through a cohesive visual identity, bold colors, and expressive typography, the designs create a fresh, approachable aesthetic that stands out on shelves. The project explores how a digital-first brand can grow across digital and physical touch-points to make mixology more accessible, engaging, and fun.
